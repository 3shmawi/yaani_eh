Read a name and had no idea what it is? Yaani Eh explains anything in plain words, the way a friend would: with a photo, one short line that says what it really is, and how to say it.

It starts with café drinks: Flat White, Cortado, Affogato, Cold Brew… New sections arrive in the app as they're ready, no update needed.

WHAT IT IS, IN WORDS YOU KNOW
Everything with its name as you see it written, its Arabic spelling, and one short line that says what it is: "strong milky coffee in a small cup", "coffee, milk and ice in a blender". No jargon, and never another strange word to explain it.

SEE WHAT'S IN THE CUP
For drinks, "How it's made" draws the cup to scale, layer by layer, so you can see that a flat white is mostly milk with a thin foam, and an americano is mostly water. Then the steps a good café follows, each with its amount.

MAKE IT AT HOME
Step by step shows one step at a time in big text, so you can follow it in your kitchen.

HEAR THE NAME
Not sure how to say "Affogato"? Hear it the way people say it, and the original pronunciation.

SEARCH BY DESCRIPTION
Type what's on your mind: "something cold with chocolate", "strong but not bitter". Search understands descriptions in Arabic and English, even with Franco spelling.

KEEP THE ONES YOU LIKE
Tap the heart to save it. Long-press any card for a quick look without leaving the page.

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything downloads once and works without internet. Favorites stay on your phone. No account needed.

On phones and tablets. Arabic and English, made for Egypt. Calories are approximate.
