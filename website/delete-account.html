Delete your Yaani Eh data

Yaani Eh has no accounts: there is nothing to sign up for, so there is no account to delete.

Data on your phone

Your favorites, settings and the downloaded menu live only on your phone. Delete the app and they are gone. This also resets the random install ID used for analytics.

Data we hold

We hold only anonymous usage and crash data (see the privacy policy). It isn't linked to your name or email, so we can't look it up from them. If you'd still like us to delete what we can, email us and we'll reply within 7 days.

Request deletion by email
Requests are completed within 30 days.
