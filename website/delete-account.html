Your data in Yaani Eh

Yaani Eh has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

On your phone

Your favorites, settings and the downloaded content are stored only on your phone. Delete the app and they are gone for good.

What we hold

Only usage analytics and crash reports that are not linked to you (no name, email or phone number), kept as long as needed to improve the app, then deleted. See the privacy policy.

Ask us

For any question or request about your data, email us. We reply within 7 days.
